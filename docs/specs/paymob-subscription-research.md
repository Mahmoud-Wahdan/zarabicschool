# Paymob documents the plumbing but not renewal outcomes

Paymob's Subscription Module will charge a saved card automatically on a fixed day-count schedule. It tells you about each renewal through a subscription callback, but **no public source shows a renewal payload**. Only one sample callback is published (`trigger_type: "suspended"`). It carries the subscription record, a `trigger_type` and a body-level `hmac`, and **no transaction id, order id, charge amount or failure reason**. The docs do name three renewal triggers: "Successful Transaction", "Failed Transaction" and "Failed Overdue Transaction". They do not say whether a renewal also fires the standard TRANSACTION processed callback. They also leave out the retry count and timing behind `retrial_days`, the state a subscription ends in after retries run out, any webhook redelivery policy, and whether a refund or void changes the subscription. The pieces that are documented are the plan fields, `register_webhook`, and the pull endpoints `/last-transaction` and `/transactions`. Those endpoints return renewal transactions marked `api_source: "SUBSCRIPTION"`. In practice, a merchant should treat the subscription callback as a nudge, settle each renewal by pulling the transaction by id, and run a daily reconciliation sweep. Before relying on the module, the merchant must get written answers to the gaps listed at the end through ticket EG-2026-94026. Evidence comes from three official sources: the developer portal, Paymob's own Postman collection (samples dated 2024-09 to 2026-01), and Paymob's official AI Integration Skill repo. One community Laravel package appears only as weak evidence. The portal pages were read through a fetch tool that summarises, so some portal quotes are short fragments. The Postman and GitHub material was read raw and is quoted exactly.

## Renewal callbacks name three triggers but publish zero renewal payloads

**CONFIRMED.** Paymob's "Subscription Callback and HMAC Calculation" page has a trigger table that maps "Next billing cycle succeeds" to `"Successful Transaction"`, "Failed deduction" to `"Failed Transaction"`, and "Failed retrial deduction" to `"Failed Overdue Transaction"`. The same table also lists `"Subscription Created"`, `"suspended"`, `"canceled"`, `"resumed"`, `"updated"`, `"add_secondry_card"` (sic), `"change_primary_card"`, `"delete_card"` and `"register_webhook"`. The callback is a POST to the registered webhook "containing the updated subscription data". The HMAC arrives as a **body field `hmac`**, not as the query parameter used on transaction callbacks ([CB-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/hmac-calculation-for-subscription-callback)). Paymob's official AI skill shows how to verify it: compute SHA-512 HMAC over `f"{body['trigger_type']}for{body['subscription_data']['id']}"`, e.g. `"Subscription Createdfor12345"` ([AI-SKILL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/Paymob-AI-Integration-Skill/main/skills/paymob-integration/references/advanced-features.md)). This signs only the trigger name and the subscription id. Nothing about the charge is covered by the signature.

**The only sample is the "suspended" one.** It has `paymob_request_id` (a UUID), `trigger_type`, `hmac` (128 hex chars) and a `subscription_data` object. That object holds `id`, `client_info`, `frequency` (365), `name`, `reminder_days`, `retrial_days`, `plan_id`, `state` ("suspended"), `amount_cents`, `starts_at`, `next_billing`, `reminder_date`, `ends_at`, `resumed_at`, `suspended_at`, `webhook_url`, `integration` and `initial_transaction` ([CB-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/hmac-calculation-for-subscription-callback)). The sample's `frequency: 365` is not a value the plan API accepts (see the plan section), which suggests it is an old or hand-edited sample. **NOT DOCUMENTED:** the body of any of the three renewal triggers. That includes whether they add a transaction id, the charged amount or a decline reason, and whether `paymob_request_id` stays the same across redeliveries so it could serve as an idempotency key. The Postman collection does not fill the gap. The strings "hmac" and "trigger" appear **zero times** in its 93 KB ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)).

Our two research notes disagree on this point, and the conflict resolves cleanly. One note found no public mention of "Failed Overdue Transaction" or of "suspended" as a callback type. That search covered Postman and the transaction-callback pages, not the subscription callback page. The callback page does list both triggers, so they are confirmed. Their payloads are not.

## Renewal charges exist as ordinary transactions you can pull, but nothing says they are pushed

**NOT DOCUMENTED:** whether a MOTO auto-deduction fires the standard TRANSACTION processed callback (`type: "TRANSACTION"`, `obj`), to which URL, and with what contents. The general callback page says that callback is sent "after the payment or after any action on the payment". It never mentions subscriptions or MOTO, and its samples show only `api_source` values "OTHER" and "IFRAME" ([Transaction callbacks](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/manage-callback/transaction-callbacks)). `notification_url` is documented only for the enrolment intention, where it "overrides the Transaction Processed Callback URL configured in your Integration ID settings" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). Paymob's own sources also contradict each other about where subscription events are sent. The AI skill says "Subscription events POST to your `notification_url`" ([AI-SKILL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/Paymob-AI-Integration-Skill/main/skills/paymob-integration/references/advanced-features.md)). Postman and the plan docs say they go to the plan's `webhook_url`, "The endpoint that will receive updates about the subscriptions related to this plan" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)).

**CONFIRMED:** renewals are real transaction objects that you can fetch. In a sample dated 2026-01, Postman's `GET /api/acceptance/subscriptions/{id}/transactions` returns paginated results. Each result has `"id": 395954050` and `"order": 449255221`, plus `"amount_cents": 1000`, `"success": true`, `"pending": false`, `"is_3d_secure": false`, `"integration_id": 4565330` and `"api_source": "SUBSCRIPTION"`. It also has `"source_id": 7400`, which equals the subscription id in the URL, and `"created_at": "2026-01-10T00:01:51.305855"`, a charge just after midnight. `GET .../last-transaction` shows a failed renewal with `"success": false`, `"error_occured": true` and `"txn_response_code": "ERROR"`, with the message "Merchant acquirer relationship does not support this Merchant Transaction Source". Postman lists its use cases as "Verify successful payment" and "Investigate recent failed payment". You can also go the other way, from a renewal to its subscription: "Auto-deduction (MOTO) transaction `GET /api/acceptance/subscriptions?transaction=323746322`" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). Paymob never defines `source_id` as the subscription id. That reading rests on one sample. No renewal sample shows `merchant_order_id` or `special_reference`, so the merchant reference set at enrolment cannot be assumed to carry over to renewals.

## Retries, final state and redelivery are the largest blanks

**CONFIRMED, but only at the level of a label.** `retrial_days` is described as "Define the days on which the subscription will be attempted again in case of a failure to collect the previous subscription amount". A Postman comment says "Specify the days for retrying the subscription payment after a failed attempt" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json); [Create Plan](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/create-subscription-plan)). The overview promises "retries for failed payments" ([Overview](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/payments-and-features/core-features/subscriptions)). The docs do not explain any of the following, so each is **NOT DOCUMENTED**: whether `retrial_days: 2` means one retry two days later or a retry on each of two days; the time of day; the default when the field is omitted; whether each retry creates a new transaction or order id; and what `next_billing` does after a failed cycle. Each card on a subscription carries a `failed_attempts` counter, but it is never explained ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)).

**NOT DOCUMENTED:** the final state after the last failed retry. The documented states are "active, suspended, cancelled", and the cancel response spells it `"canceled"` ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). A "Failed Overdue Transaction" trigger and a "suspended" sample both exist, but no page links them: nothing says that exhausted retries suspend the subscription. Backup cards make this murkier. After change-primary-card, "All future billing cycles will charge this card first" and the "Previous primary card becomes a backup" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). "Charge first" implies Paymob falls back to backup cards, but the fallback order, its timing and any promotion of a backup to primary are never stated.

**NOT DOCUMENTED:** webhook redelivery. The subscription callback page, the register-webhook page and the general transaction-callback pages give no retry count, interval, timeout or required response code ([CB-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/hmac-calculation-for-subscription-callback); [REG-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/register-webhook); [Transaction callbacks](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/manage-callback/transaction-callbacks)). The AI skill only hedges. It says "Paymob's retries give you a chance to fix it" and that "webhook senders typically retry on non-2xx or timeout", and its reference code returns 401 when the HMAC does not match ([AI-SKILL hmac-verification.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/Paymob-AI-Integration-Skill/main/skills/paymob-integration/references/hmac-verification.md)). That is advice, not a contract. No documented way exists to ask Paymob to resend a missed subscription callback. `retrial_days` re-attempts the card charge. It has nothing to do with webhook delivery.

## register_webhook works per subscription, despite Postman's variable name

**CONFIRMED:** the endpoint is `POST {base_url}/api/acceptance/subscriptions/{id}/register_webhook`, sent with `Authorization: Bearer <auth token>` (the token lasts 60 minutes) and a body of `{"url": "..."}`. Postman describes it as "register or update a webhook URL for an existing subscription". Its path variable is named `{{subscription_plan_id}}`, but the sample response is a subscription (`"id": 368`, `"plan_id": 140`, `"state": "active"`, `webhook_url` set) ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). The portal names the parameter `subID` and documents a 401 for bad credentials ([REG-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/register-webhook)). Calling it fires a `"register_webhook"` callback ([CB-DOC](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/hmac-calculation-for-subscription-callback)). For blanket coverage, the plan-level `webhook_url` is the main mechanism. The plan docs say "You need to set the parameter 'webhook_url' while creating the plan request" and that you will receive the response "on any action (subscription level only)" ([Create Plan](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/create-subscription-plan)). **NOT DOCUMENTED:** whether changing a plan's `webhook_url` reaches subscriptions that already exist. Official sources also disagree on details. The AI skill writes `/subscription_plans` with an underscore and uses `Authorization: Token {SECRET_KEY}`, while Postman uses `/subscription-plans` with a hyphen and a Bearer token ([AI-SKILL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/Paymob-AI-Integration-Skill/main/skills/paymob-integration/references/advanced-features.md)).

## Refunds and voids are generic and leave the subscription untouched on paper

**CONFIRMED:** there is no refund or void endpoint specific to subscriptions. Renewal transactions carry the standard flags `is_refunded`, `is_voided`, `refunded_amount_cents`, `is_refund`, `is_void`, `has_parent_transaction` and `parent_transaction` ([Last Transaction](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/subscription-actions/last-transaction-subscription)). `refunded_amount_cents` is "The total of the refunded amount. (The payment transaction can have more than one partial refund transaction)" ([Transaction callbacks](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/manage-callback/transaction-callbacks)). "Refunds cannot exceed the original transaction amount" ([Refund](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/manage-payment-apis/refund)). A void is "Mostly... available on the same business day, before settlement occurs" ([Void](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/manage-payment-apis/void)). Neither page mentions subscriptions. **NOT DOCUMENTED:** whether a refund or void changes `state`, `next_billing` or the deduction count; whether refunding the initial 3DS payment affects the subscription; and whether either event reaches the subscription webhook. Until Paymob says otherwise, assume a refund changes nothing on the subscription and call suspend or cancel yourself. Those lifecycle actions are well specified. Suspend stops charging: "No charges will be made until subscription is resumed". Resume "Resumes billing cycle from next scheduled date". Cancel "Stops all future billing immediately" and "cannot be undone" ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). **NOT DOCUMENTED:** what resume does when `next_billing` is already in the past, and whether missed cycles are charged retroactively.

## Plan fields: fixed day counts, two amount modes, a reminder email

The table below gives each field's documented status. Unless noted, every quote comes from the Postman collection ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)) or the Create Plan page ([Create Plan](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/create-subscription-plan)).

| Field | Status | What is documented | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| `frequency` (required) | CONFIRMED | "Values in numbers are (7, 15, 30, 60, 90, 180, 360)"; an invalid value returns 400 `"\"5\" is not a valid choice."`; "For 1 month plan, send `30`" | No calendar-month option, so monthly billing drifts; the callback sample shows 365 |
| `integration` (required) | CONFIRMED | "The MIGS Moto Integration ID, which will be used for upcoming transactions (auto deduction)" | — |
| `plan_type` | CONFIRMED, conflicting | Portal: "accepts the values (rent)"; Postman: "(rent, installment, purchase, bundle, merchant_subscription, and other). The default is 'rent'" | Behavioural difference NOT DOCUMENTED |
| `amount_cents` | CONFIRMED | "The amount of the renewal payment"; a plan update applies "for the newly created subscription" | Existing subscriptions are repriced through the per-subscription PUT |
| `use_transaction_amount` | CONFIRMED | "If enabled, the initial payment... will count as one of the deductions, and renewal payments will match the amount of that first transaction. If not, the first payment will only be for saving the end user's card"; default false | — |
| `starts_at` / `next_billing` | CONFIRMED (fields), rule NOT DOCUMENTED | Without `subscription_start_date`, "the subscription will start immediately". Sample: starts_at 2023-11-28, next_billing 2023-12-12 (frequency 7). Future-start sample: both 2024-09-25. `next_billing` can be edited with PUT | The formula, the timezone and the charge time (the sample charged at 00:01) |
| `reminder_days` | CONFIRMED | "send a notification to the customer to pay (currently supporting email notifications)"; reminder_days null gives reminder_date null | Email content and language; whether 0 is valid |
| `retrial_days` | CONFIRMED (label only) | See the retries section | Semantics NOT DOCUMENTED |
| `number_of_deductions` | CONFIRMED | "The default value is null" | How it interacts with `ends_at` and with failed cycles |
| `is_active` | CONFIRMED | "whether the plan will be created will be active or paused. The default value is true" | — |
| `webhook_url` | CONFIRMED | Set on plan creation; "subscription level only" | Whether it propagates on update |

The portal types `reminder_days`, `retrial_days` and `number_of_deductions` as strings, while Postman types them as integers. The portal also shows an undocumented `fee` field ([Create Plan](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/create-subscription-plan)).

## Enrolment runs through the Intention API, and the legacy Accept path is unverified

**CONFIRMED:** the documented way to enrol is "one 3DS transaction to save the customer's card" ([Create Subscription](https://developers.paymob.com/paymob-docs/developers/subscription/create-subscription)). It uses `POST /v1/intention/` with `Authorization: Token sk_...`, a `subscription_plan_id`, and "only a **3DS card Integration ID**", then Unified Checkout at `https://accept.paymob.com/unifiedcheckout/?publicKey=...&clientSecret=...`. Postman notes "Once the transaction completed successfully , the subscription will be created." A secondary card is added through the same intention, using `subscriptionv2_id` ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). **NOT DOCUMENTED in current docs:** enrolment through the legacy Accept flow (`/api/acceptance/payment_keys` plus an iframe). The legacy page at docs.paymob.com could not be fetched because of a TLS certificate mismatch. Search snippets suggest a payment-key request once carried `subscription_plan_id`, but that is unverified and should not be relied on. The AI skill adds that "Subscriptions are available to selected merchants — Paymob must enable the feature on the account" ([AI-SKILL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/Paymob-AI-Integration-Skill/main/skills/paymob-integration/references/advanced-features.md)).

## What Postman and community code add

Paymob's Postman collection is the best single source for endpoints and transaction objects. Its tree covers auth, plan CRUD with suspend and resume, the intention, subscription get, update, suspend, resume and cancel, `last-transaction`, `transactions`, `card-tokens`, `delete-card`, `change-primary-card` and `register_webhook`. It contains **no callback samples of any kind** ([POSTMAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PaymobAccept/API-Postman-Collections/main/Paymob%20Subscription%20Module%20API.postman_collection.json)). No official SDK parses subscription callbacks. The one community package found, ahmed-abd0/laravel-paymob, shows what goes wrong without a spec. It replaces HMAC with a URL secret because "the supplied subscription collection does not define a subscription-specific HMAC calculation" ([packagist](https://packagist.org/packages/ahmed-abd0/laravel-paymob)). It recognises a subscription webhook only when `plan_id` and `state` are top-level keys ([WebhookType.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahmed-abd0/laravel-paymob/main/src/Enums/WebhookType.php)). That is not the shape of the official `subscription_data` envelope. It also matches renewal TRANSACTION callbacks on keys such as `merchant_order_id`, which do not appear in Paymob's renewal samples ([WebhookProcessor.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahmed-abd0/laravel-paymob/main/src/Webhooks/WebhookProcessor.php)). Treat it as a caution, not a reference. Its route `/api/acceptance/subscriptions/{id}/register_webhook` does match the official one ([Subscriptions.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahmed-abd0/laravel-paymob/main/src/Resources/Subscriptions.php)).

## What to verify with Paymob under ticket EG-2026-94026

The documentation supports one integration that holds up without Paymob's help. Verify the body `hmac` on every subscription callback, return 2xx quickly, and use the callback only as a trigger. Then pull `/subscriptions/{id}/last-transaction` and settle by transaction `id`, checking `success`, `pending`, `amount_cents`, `api_source == "SUBSCRIPTION"` and `source_id`. Back this with a daily sweep after midnight over `/subscriptions?next_billing=` and `/transactions`. That design tolerates duplicate callbacks, missing callbacks and missing transaction fields. To go beyond it, for example to grant access straight from the callback or to drive dunning and refunds automatically, the merchant needs written answers from Paymob on the points below.

| # | Question for EG-2026-94026 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full sample JSON for "Successful Transaction", "Failed Transaction" and "Failed Overdue Transaction" callbacks. Do they include the transaction id, the charged amount and a decline reason? | Decides whether the callback alone can settle a renewal |
| 2 | Does each MOTO renewal also fire a TRANSACTION processed callback? To which URL (the MOTO integration's callback, `notification_url`, or none)? Is `obj.source_id` always the subscription id? | Avoids double settlement or silent misses; resolves the AI skill vs Postman conflict |
| 3 | Is `paymob_request_id` unique per event and stable across redeliveries? | Choice of idempotency key |
| 4 | Webhook redelivery: count, interval, timeout and the response codes treated as success. Can a missed callback be resent? | Sizing of the reconciliation sweep |
| 5 | `retrial_days` semantics: number of attempts, spacing, time of day, default, and whether each attempt gets a new transaction or order id | Dunning timeline and customer messaging |
| 6 | Subscription `state` and `next_billing` after the last retry fails. Is it auto-suspended or cancelled? Does a failed cycle count toward `number_of_deductions`? | Access revocation logic |
| 7 | Are backup cards charged automatically when the primary fails? In what order, and is a successful backup promoted to primary? | Predicting which card is charged |
| 8 | Effect of refund or void (renewal or initial 3DS) on `state`, `next_billing` and the deduction count, and whether a subscription callback fires | Refund workflow |
| 9 | Behaviour of resume when `next_billing` is in the past. Are missed cycles back-charged? | Prevents surprise charges |
| 10 | `next_billing` rule (starts_at + frequency days?), the timezone, and the deduction time; whether `frequency: 30` ever aligns to calendar months; why the callback sample shows 365 | Monthly package alignment |
| 11 | Behavioural meaning of `plan_type` values; whether `webhook_url` changes on a plan reach existing subscriptions | Plan design |
| 12 | Whether legacy Accept enrolment (`payment_keys` + iframe with `subscription_plan_id`) is supported, or whether the Intention/Unified Checkout flow is required; whether the module is enabled on this account | Enrolment build path |

## Conclusion

The documentation gap falls on the most critical part of a recurring-billing integration. Paymob documents how to set a subscription up, but almost nothing about what happens when a renewal charge succeeds, fails or is reversed. The signature covers only the trigger name and the subscription id, so even a verified callback proves only that something happened to that subscription. It does not prove that money moved. The defensible stance is pull-based settlement, with callbacks treated as wake-up signals. With that design, the ticket answers become refinements rather than blockers. The two exceptions are questions 6 and 2, the final state after failures and whether a TRANSACTION callback also fires. Get those answered before going live, because they decide whether a customer quietly keeps access after their card fails, or is charged and credited twice.
