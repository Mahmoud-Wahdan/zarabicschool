## English

I've been building something I'm proud of: **Zarabicschool**, a full platform for an online academy that teaches Arabic and Quran to students in many countries.

One academy, five kinds of users (Admin, Supervisor, teachers, guardians, students). Each one needs a different view, in their own time zone.

What it does:
✅ Online admissions: one approval creates the whole family's accounts
✅ One-to-one and group scheduling, with week and month calendars for every role
✅ Students join through the platform link; real Zoom links are never shared
✅ A report per student after every session, in the student's language, approved by a supervisor
✅ Invoices and online payments (Paymob), session credits and teacher payroll
✅ Automatic WhatsApp messages (reminders, sign-in details, reports) with retries when a send fails
✅ Complaints, session ratings and academy-wide announcements
✅ Arabic and English (RTL), light and dark mode, mobile-ready

Security and quality from day one:
🔒 Role checks on every page and API, full tenant isolation, login lockout, OWASP Top 10 checks
🧪 842 code tests and 122 real-browser tests with zero skipped, including an accessibility scan of every page for every role

Stack: Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Prisma · Tailwind · Playwright · Jest · Paymob · WhatsApp (OpenWA)

A small pilot with real families and teachers starts soon. Running an online academy? Let's talk 🙌
